EWADA team members are well-presented in [the 4tth Solid Symposium](https://sosy2026.eu), taking place in London, on 30 April and 1 May 2026.

Solid Symposium is the leading annual event, gathering researchers and practitioners sharing the passiion of developing and deploying solid-based applications.


The event was opened by Nigel's keynote speech on ``How we are going to make Solid work in the world we currently live in?''. It also features a fireside conversation with our Sir Tim Berners-Lee, chaired by Inrupt's CEO, John Bruce.

The event co-located with the first Solid Hackathon hosted in London, [Solid26](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/solid26-preview-building-personal-apps-and-ai-agents-tickets-1986060301826).

The Open Data Institute (ODI) has launched an open call for non-commercial projects using Solid for social good. Six projects will be selected for six months of support, including help from Solid engineers, hosted development environments and communications support.

The call welcomes charities, non-profits, academic institutions, civil society organisations and individuals. No previous experience with Solid is required. Support is provided in kind; commercial projects and organisations are not eligible.

**Applications close on 30 September 2026 at 17:00 BST.** The supported work period runs from 2 November 2026 to 30 April 2027.

[Read the ODI's Solid open call announcement for eligibility details and how to apply](https://theodi.org/news-and-events/blog/solid-open-call/).

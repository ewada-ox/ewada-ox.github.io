**CUPA: Towards Computer-Using Personal Agents**, co-authored by EWADA DPhil student Jesse Wright, has been accepted for publication in the *Semantic Web* journal.

The paper proposes Computer-Using Personal Agents (CUPAs), which combine the ability to interact with interfaces such as web browsers with access to a Personal Knowledge Graph controlled by the user. This gives agents structured personal context for automating tasks while respecting the user's data-sharing policies and preferences. The approach also supports collaboration between agents acting for different people.

The authors are Piero A. Bonatti, John Domingue, Anna Lisa Gentile, Andreas Harth, Olaf Hartig, Aidan Hogan, Katja Hose, Ernesto Jimenez-Ruiz, Deborah L. McGuinness, Ian Pickup, Chang Sun, Aisling Third, Ruben Verborgh and Jesse Wright.

**A Semantic Resource Suite for Privacy Policy Formalization**, by Rui Zhao, Vladyslav Melnychuk, Jesse Wright, Jun Zhao and Nigel Shadbolt, has been accepted to the Resource Track of the [25th International Semantic Web Conference (ISWC 2026)](https://iswc2026.semanticweb.org/).

The paper presents a suite of semantic resources for formalising privacy policies. Many congratulations to Rui and his co-authors!

ISWC 2026 takes place in Bari, Italy, from 25–29 October 2026. The paper is listed in the Resource Track on the [conference's accepted papers page](https://iswc2026.semanticweb.org/program/acceptedpapers).
